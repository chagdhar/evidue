Cloud Platform Service Level Agreement

Customer: Apex Manufacturing
Provider: CloudCore Infrastructure
Effective: 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31
Monthly service fee: $8,500.00

1. UPTIME COMMITMENT
CloudCore guarantees 99.9% monthly uptime for all production services. Uptime is measured as the percentage of total minutes in the calendar month during which the service was available.

2. DOWNTIME EXCLUSIONS
Scheduled maintenance windows (communicated at least 72 hours in advance), force majeure events, and downtime caused by Customer's own systems are excluded from the uptime calculation.

3. SERVICE CREDITS
If monthly uptime falls below 99.9%, Customer is entitled to a service credit:
- 99.0% to 99.9%: 10% of monthly fee
- 95.0% to 99.0%: 25% of monthly fee
- Below 95.0%: 50% of monthly fee
Service credits are applied against the next invoice and do not exceed 50% of the monthly fee in any single month.

4. CREDIT REQUEST PROCEDURE
Customer must submit a credit request within 30 calendar days of the end of the affected month. The request must include the dates and times of observed downtime and any supporting evidence.

5. RESPONSE TIME
CloudCore will respond to severity-1 incidents within 15 minutes. Severity-2 incidents within 1 hour. Severity-3 incidents within 4 hours.

6. INVOICE AND PAYMENT
CloudCore will invoice Customer monthly in advance. Payment is due within 30 calendar days of invoice date.
