Open `/demo`. “This is synthetic, deterministic operational data for Acme
Commerce reconciling Nova Support AI's June invoice. The vendor submitted
$15,000 for 10,000 resolved outcomes. Before any calculation, Evidue makes the
contract executable: seven-day recontacts, 24-hour human corrections, two-hour
downstream completion, attribution and account/action evidence. These are
customer-owned logs, not a model's judgment. Click Run reconciliation. Evidue
checks every claimed line and produces $12,480 as the correct payable amount,
with a $2,520 deduction across 1,680 traceable disputes. Filter disputed
outcomes and open OUT-004821: the AI started a refund, the processor rejected
it, the two-hour window expired, and a human completed it later. The vendor
claimed resolved; the contractual determination is disputed. Finally download
the line-level CSV, evidence JSON and summary. One invoice enters; one
defensible payable amount leaves.”
