# Apply for the Evidue beta

## Form introduction

Evidue verifies outcome-priced AI vendor invoices against the contract and the
customer’s own operational evidence.

We are looking for teams that buy, sell, review, approve, or reconcile
outcome-priced AI services. Do not submit confidential contract or invoice data
through this form.

## Required questions

1. **What is your name?** — Short text
2. **What is your work email?** — Email
3. **What company do you work for?** — Short text
4. **Which best describes your role?**
   - Finance, procurement, or accounts payable
   - Support or CX operations
   - AI vendor or billing infrastructure
   - Engineering or product
   - Founder or executive
   - Other
5. **Does your company currently pay any AI vendor based on outcomes?**
   - Yes, and I review or approve the billing
   - Yes, but I do not review the billing
   - We are evaluating outcome-based pricing
   - No
   - I am an AI vendor
6. **How are those charges currently verified?**
   - We trust the vendor’s report
   - We manually inspect samples
   - We reconcile exports with internal systems
   - We built an internal process
   - We use another product
   - We do not verify them
   - Not applicable
7. **What is hardest about verifying those invoices?** — Long text, optional
8. **Would you share a redacted contract, invoice, or sample export for a private audit?**
   - Yes
   - Maybe
   - No
9. **Would you be willing to speak for 20 minutes?**
   - Yes
   - Maybe
   - No

## Consent text

Your responses will be used only for Evidue product research and beta outreach.
Do not include confidential customer data, contract language, credentials, or
invoice files.

## Completion message

Thank you. I will review your response personally and contact the most relevant
beta participants.

## Recommended hidden fields

Add these optional Tally hidden fields: `source`, `campaign`, and `demo_version`.
Suggested source values are `hacker_news`, `yc_demo`, and `direct_outreach`.
The form must work when no tracking parameters are present.
