# Product Principles

1. **Money is deterministic.** No LLM call is permitted in reconciliation.
2. **Contract language is the source.** Every material executable rule and settlement term must remain traceable to exact source text.
3. **Interpretation is proposed, never self-approved.** Generated AIR versions are immutable proposals until a human approves them.
4. **Uncertainty is visible.** Missing, conflicting, or non-authoritative evidence becomes `needs_review`, never an invented fact.
5. **Evidence authority matters.** A vendor assertion is not silently upgraded to a customer system of record.
6. **Absence requires completeness.** A missing event can prove absence only when the relevant export is explicitly complete and the evidence capability supports that claim.
7. **Identity is a financial control.** Heuristic matches cannot affect money until confirmed.
8. **History is append-only.** Reconciliations and approved AIR versions are never silently rewritten.
9. **A normal user should not need compiler vocabulary.** Advanced AIR/proof/fact internals are available for audit but kept out of the primary workflow.
10. **Demo convenience cannot weaken product invariants.** Offline fixtures exercise the same AIR approval and deterministic-runtime boundaries as the product path.
